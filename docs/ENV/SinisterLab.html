Sinister Lab

ENV_SinisterLab

Sinister Lab darkened and non-reflective with wide beveled panel lines

inspired by KimKim's comic style overhaul alterations

the original artwork of the Sinister Lab created by Julia Brasil and John Dawson

IMAGES

DOWNLOAD

  • DOWNLOAD Sinister Lab - Dark Purple = recommended .zip mod TexturePack for MHO Extended Mod Manager
  • DOWNLOAD Sinister Lab - Dark Purple UPK = .zip mod of edited UPK ( for Krisan Thyme Mod Manager)

INSTALL

Install the TexturePack .zip with the MHO Extended Mod Manager (or the Krisan Thyme ModManager):

  1. Click Install Mod... and select the downloaded .zip
  2. Check the mod ON
  3. Click the Apply Changes button

PROCESS

  • the Floor spec altered to not display the cubemap by setting B channel to black
  • diffuse darkened the white panel areas and brightened the black panel areas
  • widening the bevels of the floor tiles , stylistically choosing to bake the lighting on the texture by 2d bevel + emboss from panel mask .
  • Emphasizing the panel lines further by multiplying Ambient Occlusion onto diffuse

approximate panel lines

  • initially attempted using the spec texture R (AO) to extract lines however it was from low resolution compressed DXT1 source
  • secondly attempted to approximate the design pattern programmatically to create precise lines at higher resolutions
  • the result shown above on the lower right
